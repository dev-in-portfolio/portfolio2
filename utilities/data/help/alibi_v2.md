# Alibi (Logged & Verified) - User Guide

Alibi is your comprehensive kitchen inventory management tool. It tracks Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), net sales, exceptions, and allows for rapid data entry.

## Features
- **Dashboard**: View high-level stats (COGS%, Purchases, End Inventory, exceptions).
- **Counting**: Perform physical inventory counts efficiently.
- **Invoices**: Log purchases and track ingredient/product costs.
- **Recipes**: Manage kitchen recipes and ingredient usage.
- **Reports**: Generate detailed financial and operational reports.
- **Quick Entry**: Rapidly log items you just bought or found.

## Step-by-Step Usage
1. **Dashboard Overview**: Start on the Dashboard to review this month's quick stats and check for any exceptions that need fixing.
2. **Start Counting**: Click "Start Counting" on the dashboard or navigate to the **Counting** tab to input your current inventory levels.
3. **Enter Invoices**: Use the **Invoices** tab to add new deliveries. Enable "Quick Entry" for faster logging with vendor chips.
4. **Manage Month**: Use the top right selector to switch between months or click "+ Month" to start a new period.
5. **Data Backup**: Use the **Export** button to download a full JSON backup of your data, and **Import** to restore it.

## Troubleshooting
- **Data Not Saving**: The app auto-saves to your local browser storage. Ensure you are not in Incognito mode, or export your backup frequently.
- **Exceptions Found**: If the dashboard lists exceptions, click "Review in Reports" to fix mismatched units or pricing anomalies.
