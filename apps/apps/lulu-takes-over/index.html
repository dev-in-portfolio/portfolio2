Lulu Takes Over

A lightweight music-video experience built for streaming and downloading the Lulu Takes Over video and song.

Open Live SiteBack to Apps

Project scope

  • Responsive video and audio presentation with synchronized player controls.
  • Muted-autoplay fallback behavior and reduced-motion-aware interface handling.
  • Git LFS validation that verifies the actual MP3 and MP4 objects rather than pointer files.
  • Netlify deployment with security headers, cache controls, and media-size verification.
