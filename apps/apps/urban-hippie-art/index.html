Urban Hippie Art

An art collection site and curation workspace combining public browsing with a structured review workflow.

Open Live SiteBack to Apps

Project scope

  • Collection browsing, compare picks, removal flags, title proposals, notes, and review-mode workflows.
  • Autosaved curation state and confirmed review-report snapshots stored through Netlify Blobs.
  • Exportable JSON and text artifacts for external review and archival use.
  • Backend-connected local development and smoke-testing paths for curation and reporting.
