Flagship Application

Wordz

A private, offline-first document editor built for serious writing without accounts, subscriptions, or cloud lock-in.

Back to Apps

Project scope

  • Rich-text editing with local autosave, comments, suggestions, templates, custom styles, and document snapshots.
  • DOCX, HTML, text, JSON, and Wordz-package import with DOCX and print-quality PDF export paths.
  • Integrity-checked backups, optional AES-GCM encryption, recovery snapshots, Trash restoration, and merge-oriented sync packages.
  • IndexedDB-backed storage with migration, retry, recovery, quota protection, and Android packaging through Capacitor.
