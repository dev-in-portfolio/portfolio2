Method

How a claim reaches this page

  1. The project is inspected independently, without starting from a desired job title or capability bucket.
  2. A narrow claim is written around an observable behavior, implementation, control, or release practice.
  3. Evidence dimensions and important limitations are recorded separately.
  4. Only claims with implementation, validation, runtime, or deployment evidence are published.

Evidence ledger phase 1. Additional projects remain excluded until claim-level review is complete.
