# Crossword Arena - User Guide

Crossword Arena is a premium crossword experience offering a 400-puzzle built-in pack, solo mode, and a weekly hunt feature with an expansive, intuitive grid.

## Features
- 400-puzzle built-in library.
- Solo mode for focused solving.
- Weekly Hunt feature unlocking the Meta puzzle.
- Time tracking and progress saves.

## Step-by-Step Usage
1. Open Crossword Arena from the Apps menu.
2. Select your preferred mode (Solo or Weekly Hunt) from the dashboard.
3. Choose a puzzle from the library.
4. Click on the grid and use your keyboard to fill in the answers. Use 'Across' and 'Down' buttons to toggle direction.

## Troubleshooting
- If progress doesn't save, ensure your browser is not blocking local storage.
- If the grid doesn't respond to keyboard inputs, click inside the puzzle area to regain focus.
- Refresh the page if the puzzle fails to load.
