# Magma - User Guide

Magma (MAGMA OMEGA) is an interactive 3D generative environment that lets users explore a simulated "Magma Chamber" with advanced visual controls.

## Features
- Immersive 3D generative environment.
- On-screen GUI (`lil-gui`) for deep visual customization.
- Fluid animations and atmospheric effects.

## Step-by-Step Usage
1. Open the Magma application from the menu.
2. Wait for the 3D scene to initialize and load.
3. Use your mouse to click and drag to look around the chamber.
4. Open the floating control panel (GUI) to adjust parameters like color, speed, and particle density.
5. Observe the immediate changes in the 3D environment.

## Troubleshooting
- If the application runs slowly, close other browser tabs or adjust your device's power settings for better GPU performance.
- If the control panel disappears, press 'H' or look for a small toggle button to restore it.
- A black screen usually means WebGL is not supported or failed to initialize on your device.
